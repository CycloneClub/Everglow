The world-side quest system aims to deliver a structured static experience through several key features.

(Types) All world-side quests fall into 3 categories: Main Story, Side Story, and Legend. Main story quests are the core of the game, designed to guide players through the main storyline. Side story quests are optional, offering additional content and rewards. Legend quests are a special subset of side story quests. They feature unique content and exclusive rewards, setting them apart from regular side story quests.

(Features-Main) Quests are locked before players reach certain milestones, though the unlocking requirements is visible. Some quests only become visible after specific conditions are met. Some of the quests are time-limited. Once the time limit expires, the quest becomes unavailable until a player chooses to restart it.

(UI) Quest UI consists of two parts: quest list and quest detail. In the quest list, all quests are displayed, and each quest is represented by a card. The card shows the quest name, type, state, and the time left (if applicable). Players can click on a card to view the quest detail, which includes the full description, objectives, rewards, and other information. If a quest is invisible, the card is grayed out and only shows "???" as the name, and the detail page remains empty. The UI appearance is selectable, with the marble plate technical style as the default and new themes becoming available over game progress.

(Features-Sub) Each quest is a combination of several parts, including quest name, type, state, description, objectives, rewards, and other information. The quest state can be one of the following: locked, available, completed, or failed. Objectives are the tasks that players must complete to finish the quest. The rewards are granted upon quest completion, and they include items, world state changes, or other benefits.

(Features-Objectives) Objectives can be of various types, including but not limited to: kill NPCs, collect items, consume items, reach certain locations, move for a certain distance, talk to an NPC, give items to an NPC, and complete an invasion. Two special structures are also available: parallel and branching objectives. An objective can also give players rewards that are required to complete subsequent objective(s), but these rewards might be removed after the objective is completed.
