<objective>
Close Phase 2 the way Phase 1 was closed: one consolidated deviation and blocker ledger, a populated validation map, and the complete runnable gate chain executed once with its output captured as evidence.

Output: the consolidated `02-DEVIATIONS.md`, the populated `02-VALIDATION.md`, updated inventory/ledger cross-references, a scoped ROADMAP close-out record, and the captured output of the full gate chain.
</objective>
