# Phase 2 Plan 01 — 红月水藻 Armor Set Tracer: Art-Missing Equip Registration End-to-End

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** craft and equip the 红月水藻 armor set and feel its documented magic/summon/defense/set effects, **so that** the red-algae progression branch rewards the materials I gathered with a usable four-piece set.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 `Goal` line ("The remaining non-boss item scope is implemented in dependency order, including entries and biology-design drops/materials whose design artwork or textures were not complete during Phase 1"). The roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form; this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1 plan-01 precedent).

<objective>
Prove the phase's whole chain end-to-end on one real slice, then expand it to the full set: an art-missing armor item that loads, registers an equip slot with no approved texture, applies its documented stats, is tracked in the inventory, and is gated by the new offline phase gate.

Purpose: this is the tracer for Phase 2 and it targets the phase's highest-risk architectural unknown. `02-RESEARCH.md` §Common Pitfalls 1 shows that an armor class which declares an equip type while its `_Head`/`_Body`/`_Legs` art is absent aborts mod loading — a failure the compiler cannot see. D-18 also requires these four entries to be *full* implementations, and D-02/ITEM-02 requires the set to be equippable. The tracer settles that decision after one commit instead of after four armor classes are written against a pattern that cannot load.

The tracer also stands up the two phase-wide infrastructure artifacts — `scripts/check-phase2.ps1` and `02-CLASSIFICATION.json` — on their full 21-entry contract, so every later plan adds entries to an already-green gate rather than inventing gate logic under deadline.

D-13 (fallback texture, no generated art), D-14 (identity independent of the fallback), D-17 (materials before the equipment that consumes them) and D-18 (full implementation for design-row entries) are implemented here.

Output: four `CrimsonMoonAlgae*` armor classes, the `KelpCurtainPlayer` per-player state that carries the set's heal/wet-speed/set flags, the set-flagged red-algae toxin detonation scaling, `scripts/check-phase2.ps1`, `02-CLASSIFICATION.json`, `02-DEVIATIONS.md`, and the four updated inventory rows.
</objective>
