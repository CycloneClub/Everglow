# Phase 2 Plan 02 — Biology-Design Weapon Drops: Boulder Catapult, Tendon Greatbow, Restriction Device, Reeking Bait

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** obtain and use the biology-design drop weapons that have a documented design row, **so that** the drop table contributes real weapons to my arsenal instead of only materials.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 `Goal` line. The roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form; this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1 plan-01 precedent).

<objective>
Implement the four biology-design drop weapons whose design rows are defined, in dependency order after the material-consuming armor set (D-17): the self-contained ranged launcher with a real projectile chain, the bow with its boss-damage clause, the summon restriction device, and the dragon bait summon item.

Purpose: these are the entries ITEM-01 owns — "planned natural-themed weapons, ammunition, materials, utility items, and biology-design creature drops/materials belonging to those item families". Three of the four carry designer recipes naming Phase 7 Giant Winged Dragon materials that do not exist in the repository, so the plan's discipline is to implement everything the design documents that this phase can actually build, and to record the rest as precise, actionable blockers rather than either omitting the items or writing recipes that cannot compile.

Output: `BoulderCatapult` plus `BoulderCatapult_Proj`/`BoulderCatapult_SubProj`, `TendonGreatbow` plus `TendonGreatbow_Arrow`, `RestrictionDeviceRE01`, `ReekingBait`, and the four updated inventory rows with their manifest and ledger records.
</objective>
