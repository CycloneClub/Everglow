# Phase 4 Plan 02 — Death Jade Lake: Surface & Amphibious Family (水黾, 剧毒蟾蜍, 幽光蝾螈)

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** meet the lake's surface skater, its poisonous toad and its glowing axolotl and have each behave the way the design describes, **so that** the Death Jade Lake waterline and shallows feel alive and hostile in distinct ways.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 4 `Goal` line; the roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form and this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1/2/3 plan precedent).

<objective>
Implement the three Death Jade Lake creatures whose behaviour is anchored to the waterline: the surface-only skater 水黾, the land-spawning amphibious ambusher 剧毒蟾蜍 with its two projectiles, and the underwater glow-and-moisture axolotl 幽光蝾螈.

Purpose: these three rows are where the design's three *distinct* water conditions are actually consumed — 水黾 is `水面上`, 剧毒蟾蜍 is `在地表生成` (dry land), 幽光蝾螈 is `在水下生成` — so they are the first real test of the `KelpCurtainSpawnConditions` predicates the tracer created. They are also the first test of the phase's debuff mapping: the design names 中毒, 酸性毒液 and 潮湿, and this plan must apply exactly the mapping `04-DEVIATIONS.md` §10 records (`BuffID.Poisoned`, `BuffID.Venom`, `BuffID.Wet`) rather than invent one.

D-52/D-53 (server-safe layer-gated spawning, no dependency on unbuilt terrain), D-55 (server authority plus `!Main.dedServ`), D-56/D-58 (direct drop tables; absent drops become blockers with no type reference) and OQ2's variant rule (one class plus a `NPC.localAI[]` index for colour-only variants) are implemented here. D-58's "the mod must still build and load" is exercised by 剧毒蟾蜍's and 幽光蝾螈's deliberately empty loot tables.

Output: `WaterStrider.cs`, `ToxicToad.cs`, `GlowSalamander.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/NPCs/DeathJadeLake/`, plus `ToxicToad_PoisonBubble.cs` and `ToxicToad_PoisonCloud.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/Projectiles/Enemies/`.
</objective>
