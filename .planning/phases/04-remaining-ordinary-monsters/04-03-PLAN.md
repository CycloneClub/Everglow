# Phase 4 Plan 03 — Death Jade Lake: Passive & Neutral Water Family (装甲虾, 爆弹水母, 帆鳍鳢)

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** swim past harmless shrimp and drifting jellyfish that only threaten me when they die, and meet a fish that ignores me until I provoke it, **so that** the lake reads as an ecosystem with its own rules rather than a wall of attackers.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 4 `Goal` line; the roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form and this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1/2/3 plan precedent).

<objective>
Implement the Death Jade Lake's three non-aggressive rows: the passive group-swimming 装甲虾, the passive hovering 爆弹水母 in its two stat variants plus its death explosion, and the neutral ramming 帆鳍鳢.

Purpose: these are the phase's first passivity and neutrality problems, and each has a specific trap. 装甲虾's design says it spawns in groups of 2–5, but tML has no group-spawn primitive and a naive `OnSpawn` implementation cascades; this plan pins the non-leader marker that stops it. 爆弹水母 is the phase's first stat-variant row, and `SetDefaults` is per-type, so the design's 小/大 split must be two classes rather than an `NPC.lifeMax` mutation in `OnSpawn` (OQ2, Pitfall 5) — and it is also the row whose only threat is its death blast, which is a real gameplay effect and therefore built rather than blocked. 帆鳍鳢 is the phase's first neutral row: the design's 获得仇恨 has no explicit trigger, so the plan pins one reading and records it rather than leaving the executor to guess.

D-52/D-53/D-55 (server-safe layer-gated spawning and server authority), D-56/D-58 (direct tables; absent drops become blockers) and OQ2's variant rule are implemented here.

Output: `ArmoredShrimp.cs`, `BombJellyfish.cs`, `LargeBombJellyfish.cs` and `SailfinSnakehead.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/NPCs/DeathJadeLake/`, plus `BombJellyfish_Explosion.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/Projectiles/Enemies/`.
</objective>
