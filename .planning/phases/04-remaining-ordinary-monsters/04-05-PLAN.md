# Phase 4 Plan 05 — Death Jade Lake Identity Shells (荧光水螅, 巨型虎虾, 炮弹藤壶)

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** be able to encounter every creature the design lists, even the ones whose design is still just a name, **so that** the layer's roster is complete and nothing silently disappears from the plan.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 4 `Goal` line; the roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form and this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1/2/3 plan precedent).

<objective>
Give the three design rows that carry only a heading a loadable, gated, documented identity-only `ModNPC` class each, with a precise blocker instead of invented behaviour.

Purpose: this plan is deliberately small, and that is the point. D-45 forbids inventing behaviour for a design row that has none, D-30/D-29's precedent (Phase 2's identity shells, Phase 3's `D-29 shell` label) is the shape to reuse, and D-48 requires every Phase 4 creature to load without art. A shell that is loadable, registered, layer-gated, artwork-blocked and behaviour-blocked satisfies all three, and it is what makes the row's `code_complete` marking honest at close-out: the row's code obligation is fulfilled (a content class exists and loads), while its behaviour obligation is explicitly unfinished and visible.

D-45 (identity-only shells for undefined rows), D-48/D-49 (missing-art handling and the migration), D-52/D-55 (server-safe layer-gated spawning) and D-58's build-and-load requirement are implemented here.

Output: `FluorescentHydra.cs`, `GiantTigerShrimp.cs` and `CannonBarnacle.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/NPCs/DeathJadeLake/`.
</objective>
