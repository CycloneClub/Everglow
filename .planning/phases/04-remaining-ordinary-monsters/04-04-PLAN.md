# Phase 4 Plan 04 — Death Jade Lake: Stealth, Ranged & Bottom Predators (放射虫, 覆藻章鱼, 大型覆藻章鱼)

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** be shot at by something I can see coming, ambushed by something I cannot, and grabbed by something that only lives on the lake floor, **so that** the lake's deep water carries real risk at every depth band.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 4 `Goal` line; the roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form and this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1/2/3 plan precedent).

<objective>
Implement the Death Jade Lake's three predator rows: the ranged-then-dashing 放射虫, the stealth ink-squirting 覆藻章鱼, and the water-bottom grab-and-wave mini-ambusher 大型覆藻章鱼, together with the two projectiles their primary attacks need.

Purpose: these rows carry the phase's first real *distance* logic. 放射虫 must agree with itself about whether a target is within four tiles on both client and server, 覆藻章鱼's stealth is a distance-driven `NPC.alpha` (not a client VFX system, which is recorded as an effect blocker instead of being faked), and 大型覆藻章鱼 moves its own threat into the bottom band the tracer's `IsWaterBottom` predicate already defines. They are also the rows where the region gap bites: 覆藻章鱼's design adds 森雨幽谷 to its habitat, and there is no region predicate for it, so the plan records a precise blocker rather than inventing a coordinate test (D-52/D-53).

D-46/D-47 (implement the independently-completable part; record the missing system), D-55 (server authority plus `!Main.dedServ`), D-56/D-57/D-58/D-59 (direct tables over implemented items; absent drops become blockers) and OQ4's projectile-scope rule are implemented here.

Output: `Radiolarian.cs`, `AlgaeOctopus.cs` and `LargeAlgaeOctopus.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/NPCs/DeathJadeLake/`, plus `Radiolarian_WaterBolt.cs` and `AlgaeOctopus_InkCloud.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/Projectiles/Enemies/`.
</objective>
