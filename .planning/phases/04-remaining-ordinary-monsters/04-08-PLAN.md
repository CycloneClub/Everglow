# Phase 4 Plan 08 — Valley of Lush and Moist: Passive Conch & the Mini Boss (小格普螺, 大型荆棘苔龟)

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** pick my way past a harmless snail and then be surprised by a rare stone-backed giant turtle that shells up, hammers the ground, dives and runs, **so that** the Valley has both a quiet floor and one fight worth remembering.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 4 `Goal` line; the roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form and this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1/2/3 plan precedent).

<objective>
Implement the Valley of Lush and Moist's last two rows: the passive 小格普螺 and the 大型荆棘苔龟 Mini Boss in full, with its shockwave and boulder projectiles.

Purpose: 大型荆棘苔龟 is the phase's richest single row and its biggest scope decision. The design supplies a complete four-state machine with exact timings, a reflect formula with a clamp, a tile-collision-ignoring flight and an off-screen boulder rain, so D-47's "design row complete enough" test passes and OQ5's call is full implementation — but the machine also carries the phase's sharpest state hazard, because the retracted state's defence of 999 must be restored on *every* exit path, including a target lost mid-flight. This plan therefore pins the Phase 3 `巨树人` precedent: the defence switch lives in one `EnterState`-style helper rather than in hand-written exit branches, which is what makes state 4 reachable without starting at 999.

D-47 (case-by-case judgement, recorded), D-55 (server authority plus `!Main.dedServ`), D-56/D-58/D-59 (direct tables; a TBD design drop becomes an empty table plus a blocker) and OQ5's resolution are implemented here.

Output: `SmallGuppyConch.cs` and `LargeMossyThornTurtle.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/NPCs/ValleyOfLushAndMoist/`, plus `LargeMossyThornTurtle_Shockwave.cs` and `LargeMossyThornTurtle_Boulder.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/Projectiles/Enemies/`.
</objective>
