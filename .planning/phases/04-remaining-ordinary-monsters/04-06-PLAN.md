# Phase 4 Plan 06 — Spiny Moss Court: Soldiers, Commander & Flydragon (枯木活化士兵, 王庭号令者, 布罗迪蝇蜓)

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** fight a courtyard garrison that shoots, casts, charges and flanks me, led by a commander who keeps his distance and calls in reinforcements, **so that** the Spiny Moss Court fights like a court rather than a scatter of enemies.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 4 `Goal` line; the roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form and this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1/2/3 plan precedent).

<objective>
Implement the three remaining Spiny Moss Court rows: the four-variant 枯木活化士兵 garrison, the summoning-and-rallying 王庭号令者, and the flying 布罗迪蝇蜓 in its two sizes, together with the two projectiles the soldier variants' primary attacks need.

Purpose: this is the phase's D-46/D-47 frontier. The design gives 枯木活化士兵 four complete stat rows and four distinct attack patterns, so per D-47 the independently-completable part is fully implemented — but the design also hangs its 意志高涨 buff and its morale-gated 干涸心脏 drop on a morale/command system that does not exist, so those are recorded as blockers rather than half-built (D-46). The same split governs 布罗迪蝇蜓: the flying melee creature is implementable, the 森雨幽谷 egg system is not. This plan deliberately builds the soldier classes so that a later morale system can raise their defence, damage and movement speed without reworking their AI, which is what makes the blocker a deferral rather than a dead end.

OQ2's variant rule (separate classes for stat variants), D-46/D-47 (implement the independently-completable part; record the missing system precisely), D-52/D-53/D-55 (layer-gated server-safe spawning with no terrain or region approximation) and D-56/D-58 (one wired Phase 1 drop; every absent material a blocker) are implemented here.

Output: seven creature classes under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/NPCs/SpinyMossCourt/` and two projectiles under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/Projectiles/Enemies/`.
</objective>
