# Phase 4 Plan 07 – Valley of Lush and Moist: Hazards & the Needle Caterpillar (红针洋辣子  阿萨辛覆盘子, 蛇行苔)

## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** be stung by a caterpillar that shoots needles, ambushed by a raspberry that was pretending to be scenery, and grabbed by moss that was pretending to be a plant, **so that** the Valley punishes me for walking through it without looking.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 4 `Goal` line; the roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form and this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1/2/3 plan precedent).

<objective>
Implement the Valley of Lush and Moist's three ordinary hazards: the needle-firing caterpillar that reuses the repository's existing segmented-worm AI, the buried raspberry that ambushes at a precise distance window, and the binding moss, together with the two spike projectiles.

Purpose: this plan carries the phase's one genuine cross-module reuse. 红针洋辣子's design says its AI is 同树皮刺毛虫 and its drop is 掉落同树皮刺毛虫, and the repository already holds both – an abstract coroutine-driven `Caterpillar` template in `Everglow.Function/Templates/Enemies` and the implemented `CaterpillarJuice` material – so the honest implementation reuses them rather than re-deriving a segmented worm or inventing a drop. That reuse is also why the gate's item index must span the whole `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil` tree (OQ1). The other two rows are the phase's D-46 disguise cases: their ambush *mechanics* are fully specified and are built, while the 伪装 visual system that hides them is missing and is recorded as a blocker rather than faked with an alpha trick that would be indistinguishable from a bug.

D-46 (build the independently-completable part, record the missing system), D-53 (no dependency on unbuilt terrain), D-55 (server authority plus `!Main.dedServ`), D-57/D-59 (reference implemented items; follow the design's chances) and OQ1's resolution are implemented here.

Output: `RedNeedleCaterpillar.cs`, `AssassinRaspberry.cs` and `SerpentMoss.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/NPCs/ValleyOfLushAndMoist/`, plus `RedNeedleCaterpillar_Spike.cs` and `AssassinRaspberry_Spike.cs` under `Sources/Modules/Yggdrasil/KelpCurtain/Projectiles/Enemies/`.
</objective>
