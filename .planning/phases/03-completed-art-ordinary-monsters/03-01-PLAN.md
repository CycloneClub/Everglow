## User Story

**As a** Kelp Curtain player, **I want to** meet the ordinary creatures whose art is already finished and have them spawn, fight and drop correctly inside Yggdrasil, **so that** the layer feels populated while the remaining art is being drawn.

> Planner-derived from the ROADMAP Phase 3 `Goal` line ("Ordinary creatures with complete design textures are playable across the designed Kelp Curtain contexts, while their drops are supplied by the completed item work rather than deferred into creature implementation"). The roadmap goal is not authored in user-story form; this restates it without adding scope (the Phase 1/2 plan-01 precedent).

<objective>
Establish the phase's whole chain on one real creature, then hand later plans a green gate to expand into: a repository-art creature that loads, spawns only inside the Yggdrasil Subworld, walks with a vanilla AI, reflects melee damage while spinning, drops an already-implemented Phase 1 item, is recorded in the new biology matrix, and is gated by the new offline phase gate.

Purpose: this is the Phase 3 tracer. It targets the phase's two highest-risk unknowns. First, the tranche rule: `03-RESEARCH.md` (Finding 1, Open Question 1) proposed a design-`<img>` proxy that D-26 could not literally support; D-41 supersedes it with "the repository already contains the creature's approved art", and D-42 pulls in creatures that have repository art but no design artwork marker. The tracer freezes that derivation in a machine-readable artifact after one commit instead of after four creature classes are written against a membership that later moves. Second, the spawn boundary: `NPCSpawnManager.EditSpawnPool` returns early outside the subworld (03-RESEARCH Pitfall 3), so isolation depends on the per-creature spawn gate; proving it once here is cheaper than discovering a main-world leak after four classes ship.

The tracer also stands up the phase-wide infrastructure — `03-BIOLOGY.json`, `03-BIOLOGY.md`, `scripts/check-biology.ps1` — on its full 31-row contract, so every later plan adds rows and classes to an already-green gate rather than inventing gate logic under deadline.
