# Phase 3 Plan 03 — 巨树人: Three-Range Giant Tree Enemy

<objective>
Implement the tranche's only complex creature: the rare Spiny Moss Court giant tree man, whose design row specifies a three-range state machine, a ground shockwave, a thrown boulder, and a deliberate vulnerability window after each smash.

Purpose: this is the last new creature of the repository-art tranche and the only one that needs attack projectiles. It is also the only tranche creature whose designed drops are all already implemented (巨树之臂, 硬化枯木心脏, 巨石弹射装置), so it is where the phase's drop wiring is exercised for real rather than only blocked. Splitting the class (Task 1) from the two projectiles (Task 2) keeps each task inside a single concern and lets the AI be proven against a compiled class before the projectile types exist.

D-28 (full implementation for a complete design row), D-30 (implement the independently completable part; projectiles are built here rather than deferred because nothing about them is missing information), D-31 (local `AI()` because no vanilla `aiStyle` has a three-range charge/smash/recovery state machine), D-32/D-33 (subworld-only spawn through the existing manager), D-34 (conservative defaults recorded), D-35 (`!Main.dedServ`, server authority), D-36 (direct drop table) and D-37/D-39 (only implemented items in a table) are implemented here.

Output: `GiantDandelion.cs`, `GiantDandelion_Shockwave.cs`, `GiantDandelion_Boulder.cs`, and updated `03-BIOLOGY.json`, `03-BIOLOGY.md` and `03-DEVIATIONS.md`.

**Behaviour, verbatim intent.** A rare enemy, the remains of a dead giant tree man, wandering the swamp of 刺苔庭园, actively attacking the player. States: (0) idle wander with no aggro; (1) within 5 tiles of the player — slowly raise the arm to charge and then smash the ground, the whole process taking 120 ticks, producing a high-damage, large-radius ground shockwave with roughly a 2-tile-high hit box; then a fixed 300-tick immobile recovery with the fist stuck in the floor, after which it yanks the arm up and flings a boulder forward — if the player is still within 5 tiles, repeat; (2) at 5–15 tiles — close in, and may suddenly pause for 120 ticks and raise an arm backwards, then swing both arms forward quickly bringing up a boulder, then recover the arms within 120 ticks and continue chasing unless the player is within 5 tiles, with at least 240 ticks between the two attacks of this state; (3) beyond 15 tiles with aggro — chase faster. While the fist is sunk in the floor during state 1, defense is 0 and it takes 150% damage.
